Health Sync

❤️ FitSoleil uses Apple Shortcuts to sync with Apple Health. Install each shortcut once, then run from the Shortcuts app.
Log Weight → Health
Sends your logged weight from FitSoleil to Apple Health.
Log Water → Health
Sends your daily water intake to Apple Health.
Log Cycle → Health
Syncs your cycle log to Apple Health Cycle Tracking.
Import Weight from Health
Pulls weight data from Apple Health into FitSoleil's weight log.
